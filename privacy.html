Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Everyday Money Math Demand Research handles information in connection with its website and internal research application.

1. Purpose of the Application

Everyday Money Math Demand Research is an internal research and content-planning system used to analyze demand signals relating to educational financial topics.

The system may use authorized APIs, including the YouTube Data API and Google Ads API, to retrieve public or account-authorized research information.

2. Personal Information

This website does not provide user registration, public accounts, payment processing, or forms intended to collect personal information from visitors.

The application is primarily operated locally by its owner and is not designed as a public consumer application.

3. Google API Data

Data retrieved through Google APIs is used for legitimate research, testing, analysis, and content-planning purposes.

API credentials, service-account credentials, private keys, access tokens, refresh tokens, and API keys are treated as confidential and are not intentionally published through this website.

4. YouTube Data

The application may retrieve publicly available YouTube information such as video identifiers, titles, channel information, publication dates, view statistics, engagement statistics, and video duration where made available through the YouTube Data API.

The application does not represent such information as proprietary data and does not claim that YouTube API result counts represent actual keyword search volume.

5. Google Ads Data

Where authorized, the application may use Google Ads API data for keyword and historical demand research, including historical search metrics and related market information.

This information is used internally for research and content planning.

6. Data Sharing

The project does not sell personal information or API credentials. Information obtained through APIs is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties except where required for operation of the relevant services, required by law, or permitted under the applicable API terms.

7. Hosting and Technical Logs

This informational website may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider. That provider may automatically process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, or access logs according to its own privacy practices.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep API credentials and private configuration separate from publicly accessible website content and source files intended for publication.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the project's functionality, integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the contact email listed in the project's Google Cloud application configuration.
