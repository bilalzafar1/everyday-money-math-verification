Everyday Money Math Demand Research

Everyday Money Math Demand Research is a private research and content-planning tool used to study public search and video-market data related to everyday financial questions.

The project supports research for educational content about practical financial calculations, including topics such as loans, payments, interest, saving, budgeting, affordability, and other everyday money decisions.

Purpose

The system analyzes public and authorized data from services such as the YouTube Data API and Google Ads API to better understand search interest, existing video content, historical keyword information, and related demand signals.

The application is primarily an internal research tool and is not offered as a public financial service.

Important: Everyday Money Math Demand Research does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax, legal, or credit advice. Research produced by the system is intended for educational and content-planning purposes only.

Google API Usage

The project may access Google APIs using credentials owned and controlled by the project operator. API data is used only for legitimate research, analysis, testing, and content-planning purposes.

Contact

Questions concerning this project, privacy, or its use of Google APIs may be directed to the contact email provided through the project's Google Cloud application configuration.
